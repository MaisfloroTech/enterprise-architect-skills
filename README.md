# Enterprise Architect Skills

Skills para arquitectos empresariales que ayudan a dar forma a los entregables de un caso paso a paso, en el lenguaje en que deciden los ejecutivos. Funcionan en español o en inglés. Sin números inventados.

[English version below](#english)

## 📚 Sobre este repo

Estas skills nacieron de hacer el curso **"Arquitectura Empresarial: De la Estrategia a la Tecnología"**, de **Daniel Dughman Manzur**, como apoyo para estudiar casos de arquitectura empresarial.

**Este repo no resuelve el caso.** Te ayuda a darles forma a los entregables. Resolverlo requiere estudiar, entender el contexto y considerar escenarios; estas skills te ayudan a plasmar ese trabajo en una estructura de borrador inicial, que después tú revisas, ajustas y defiendes.

## 🧰 Skills

| # | Skill | Qué hace |

## English

Skills that help enterprise architects shape the deliverables of a case step by step, in the language executives act on. They work in Spanish or English. No made-up numbers.

### 📚 About this repo

These skills came from taking the course **"Arquitectura Empresarial: De la Estrategia a la Tecnología"** by **Daniel Dughman Manzur**, as a study aid for enterprise architecture cases.

**This repo does not solve the case.** It helps you shape the deliverables. Solving it takes study, understanding the context and weighing scenarios; these skills help you put that work into a first-draft structure that you then review, adjust and defend.

### 🧰 Skills

The number in each name is the order to use them in a case.

| # | Skill | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [`1-ea-scope-and-objectives`](1-ea-scope-and-objectives/README.md#english) | Scope, general objective, objectives with an accountable owner and a deadline in days, 90-day commitment and open questions |
| 2 | [`2-ea-stakeholder-map`](2-ea-stakeholder-map/README.md#english) | Stakeholder map in political language: power, stance, interests, risks, one-to-one talking points, affinities and a 14-day survey |
| 3 | [`3-ea-diagnosis-findings`](3-ea-diagnosis-findings/README.md#english) | Key negative findings: risk, opportunity, sensitivity, how each one is handled and what happens if the risk materialises |
| 4 | [`4-ea-capabilities`](4-ea-capabilities/README.md#english) | Full architecture proposal: capability map with owners, anchoring, maturity, prioritisation, teams, decision record and a roadmap in days |
| 5 | [`5-ea-stakeholder-conversations`](5-ea-stakeholder-conversations/README.md#english) | One-to-one conversations in a single table: order, what hurts each person, finding to review, proposal, day-14 deliverables and what they gain |
| 6 | [`6-ea-governance`](6-ea-governance/README.md#english) | First committee meeting and governance: structure, workstreams, cadence, weekly report, traffic light with escalation and risk register |
| 7 | [`7-ea-next-steps-and-plan-b`](7-ea-next-steps-and-plan-b/README.md#english) | Next steps per workstream from day 15 to 90 and a plan B if the first meeting goes wrong, with a relaunch with the CEO (Chief Executive Officer) |
| 8 | [`8-ea-glossary`](8-ea-glossary/README.md#english) | Glossary: people with their job title and role, and the terms of the case; no acronym without its full name |
| — | [`ea-unified-case-excel`](ea-unified-case-excel/README.md#english) | Puts the closed points in one Excel workbook, one tab per point. It only unifies: each point is built with its own skill |
